ABOUT THE GAME

THE EXPERIENCE

Carve a Pumpkin is a Halloween-themed Roblox experience where players grow their own pumpkins, carve unique designs, and compete in voting contests. Perfect for the spooky season!

KEY FEATURES

  • • 50+ unique pumpkin seed varieties
  • • Realistic carving tools and mechanics
  • • Daily voting contests with rewards
  • • Passive income from placed pumpkins
  • • Plot customization and decorations
  • • Cross-platform play (PC, Mobile, Xbox)
